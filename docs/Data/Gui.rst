Below follows an overview of the developed graphical interface to be used in the data treatment.


SliceViewer
^^^^^^^^^^^

Slices through data binned in Qx, Qy and energy (DataSet.view_3d): a slider, the arrow keys and
the scroll wheel move through the slices, keys 0, 1 and 2 choose the slicing axis, and with
cutting on (key c) drawn lines become 1D, Q-E and constant-Q cuts.
